# Master No-Oriented Momentum

## R — 原文 (Reading)

> “Yes” and “Maybe” are often worthless. But “No” always alters the conversation.
>
> — Chris Voss, Chapter 4 — Beware “Yes”—Master “No”

---

## I — 方法论骨架 (Interpretation)

Voss reverses the usual obsession with yes. “No” protects autonomy, clarifies boundaries, and reveals real stakes. Instead of cornering someone into agreement, ask questions they can safely reject: “Is now a bad time?”, “Have you given up on…?”, “Would it be ridiculous…?” The purpose is not negativity; it is momentum through truthful boundaries.

## B — 边界 (Boundary) ★

### 不要在以下情况使用此 skill

- Do not use “Have you given up…?” as a guilt-trip in sensitive relationships.
- Do not treat every No as hidden Yes; respect hard refusal.
- Not for closing implementation; use “How” verification there.

### 作者在书中警告的失败模式

- 不要把谈判当成战斗、辩论或单纯的价格拉扯; 先发现真实约束。
- 不要追逐表面 “Yes”; 没有 “How” 的 yes 可能毫无执行力。

### 作者的盲点 / 时代局限

- 本书来自 FBI/危机谈判背景, 现实商务/亲密关系中权力、法律和长期关系约束更复杂。
- 书中战术很强, 但若脱离伦理边界, 容易滑向操控。

### 容易混淆的邻近方法论

- Use voss-execution-how when an agreement exists but may not happen.
