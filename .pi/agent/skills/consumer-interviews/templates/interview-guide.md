# Interview Guide Template — Research Dimensions

Every interview guide must include questions covering ALL of the following dimensions. Generate at least 20 questions, with 2-3 probes per question.

## Required Dimensions

| # | Dimension | Example Question Start |
|---|-----------|----------------------|
| 1 | Consumption motivation | "What first got you interested in [category]?" |
| 2 | Frequency of use/purchase | "How often do you find yourself [using/buying] [category]?" |
| 3 | Decision-making process | "Walk me through the last time you decided to [purchase/use]..." |
| 4 | Research & comparison | "Before making a decision, what do you typically look into?" |
| 5 | Alternatives & competitors | "What other options did you consider before choosing [product]?" |
| 6 | Top-of-mind brands | "When I say [category], what's the first thing that comes to mind?" |
| 7 | Aha moments | "Has there been a moment where [product/category] really clicked for you?" |
| 8 | Pain points | "What's the most frustrating part of [category/process]?" |
| 9 | Unmet needs | "If you could change one thing about [category], what would it be?" |
| 10 | Price sensitivity | "How do you think about price when it comes to [category]?" |
| 11 | Switching barriers | "What would make you switch from your current [solution]?" |
| 12 | Trust signals | "How do you decide whether to trust a [category] brand?" |
| 13 | Emotional drivers | "How does [category] make you feel? What's the emotional side?" |
| 14 | Functional drivers | "What's the practical reason you use [category]?" |
| 15 | Context of use | "Describe a typical situation where you'd reach for [category]..." |
| 16 | Market/category perceptions | "How would you describe the [category] landscape to a friend?" |
| 17 | Segment-specific behavior | [Tailored to segment — e.g., "As a parent, how do your kids influence..."] |
| 18 | Purchase triggers | "What usually prompts you to start looking for a new [solution]?" |
| 19 | Post-purchase experience | "After buying, what's been your experience with [category]?" |
| 20 | Future expectations | "What do you wish [category/product] could do that it doesn't?" |

## Question Quality Checklist

Every question must:
- Be open-ended (not yes/no)
- Be answerable from the respondent's perspective
- Avoid leading language or jargon
- Be relevant to at least one segment
- Have 2-3 follow-up probes that dig deeper
- Be conversational in tone

## Probe Patterns

Good probes follow these patterns:
- **Depth probe:** "Can you tell me more about that?"
- **Specificity probe:** "Can you give me a specific example?"
- **Emotion probe:** "How did that make you feel?"
- **Contrast probe:** "How is that different from [alternative]?"
- **Timeline probe:** "What happened next?"
- **Reason probe:** "Why do you think that is?"
- **Hypothetical probe:** "If [scenario changed], what would you do differently?"
