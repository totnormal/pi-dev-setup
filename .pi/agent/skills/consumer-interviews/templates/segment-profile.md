# Segment Profile Schema

Use this schema when creating or reviewing consumer segments. Every segment must include all fields below.

## Schema

### Identity
- **Name:** Short, descriptive segment name (e.g., "Health-Conscious Parents")
- **Description:** 1-2 sentence summary of who these consumers are

### Demographics
- **Age range:** (e.g., "30-45")
- **Income:** (e.g., "Middle to upper-middle")
- **Location:** (e.g., "Urban and suburban")
- **Family status:** (e.g., "Parents of children under 12")
- **Occupation:** (e.g., "Professional/managerial")

### Psychographics
- **Values:** What matters most to them (e.g., "Health, family time, convenience")
- **Lifestyle:** Daily patterns and routines
- **Media consumption:** Where they get information and entertainment

### Category Relationship
- **Usage frequency:** How often they engage with the product category
- **Brand awareness:** What they know about brands in the space
- **Loyalty level:** Tendency to stick with one brand vs. switch

### Needs & Motivations
- **Primary needs:** What they're trying to accomplish
- **Emotional drivers:** Feelings driving their behavior
- **Functional drivers:** Practical reasons for their choices

### Barriers & Objections
- **Purchase barriers:** What prevents them from buying
- **Switching barriers:** What keeps them with their current solution
- **Likely objections:** What they'd push back on

### Purchase Process
- **Triggers:** What initiates consideration
- **Research behavior:** How they evaluate options
- **Decision criteria:** What tips the decision
- **Price sensitivity:** How price affects their choices

### Priority
- **Priority level:** High / Medium / Low (based on research goals and business importance)

## Validation Rules

A segment is complete only if:
- [ ] Name is specific (not "General Consumers")
- [ ] Description differentiates from other segments
- [ ] Demographics are realistic and internally consistent
- [ ] At least 2 needs are identified
- [ ] At least 1 barrier or objection is identified
- [ ] At least 1 purchase trigger is identified
- [ ] Priority is assigned with justification
