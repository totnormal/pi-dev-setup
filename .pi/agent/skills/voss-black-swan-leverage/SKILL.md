# Find the Black Swan

## R — 原文 (Reading)

> “In every negotiation there are between three and five pieces of information that, were they to be uncovered, would change everything.”
>
> — Chris Voss, Chapter 10 — Find the Black Swan

---

## I — 方法论骨架 (Interpretation)

Black Swans are hidden facts that transform leverage. They may be constraints, private motives, bad information, worldview commitments, or invisible stakeholders. Finding them requires humility: treat what you know as incomplete, listen for anomalies, use labels/questions, and explore outside the formal table. The aim is not more data in general; it is the few facts that change what is possible.

## B — 边界 (Boundary) ★

### 不要在以下情况使用此 skill

- Do not invent conspiracy theories; anomalies need evidence.
- Do not use hidden leverage for blackmail or coercion.
- Some situations require accepting no deal rather than hunting endlessly.

### 作者在书中警告的失败模式

- 不要把谈判当成战斗、辩论或单纯的价格拉扯; 先发现真实约束。
- 不要追逐表面 “Yes”; 没有 “How” 的 yes 可能毫无执行力。

### 作者的盲点 / 时代局限

- 本书来自 FBI/危机谈判背景, 现实商务/亲密关系中权力、法律和长期关系约束更复杂。
- 书中战术很强, 但若脱离伦理边界, 容易滑向操控。

### 容易混淆的邻近方法论

- Use voss-active-listening for general discovery; use this when searching for game-changing facts.
- Use voss-bend-reality when facts are known but framing is weak.
