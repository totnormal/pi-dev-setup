# Trigger “That’s Right”

## R — 原文 (Reading)

> “The sweetest two words in any negotiation are actually ‘That’s right.’”
>
> — Chris Voss, Chapter 5 — Trigger the Two Words That Immediately Transform Any Negotiation

---

## I — 方法论骨架 (Interpretation)

“That’s right” is the moment the counterpart recognizes you have captured their world accurately. It is different from “yes” (which may be fake) and “you’re right” (which often dismisses you). Build it by combining labels, paraphrase, and summary of facts plus emotions. Once they say or imply “that’s right,” they become more open to re-evaluating their position.

## A2 — 触发场景 (Future Trigger) ★

### 用户会在什么情境下需要这个 skill?

1. The user needs to persuade someone who feels misunderstood.
2. The counterpart repeats the same position and ignores arguments.
3. The user wants a summary that unlocks movement.

### 语言信号 (用户的话里出现这些就应激活)

- "get them to say that’s right"
- "summarize their worldview"
- "they keep saying you’re right"
- "permission to persuade"

### 与相邻 skill 的区分

- Use voss-labeling earlier when emotions are not yet clear.
- Use voss-bend-reality after “that’s right” when shaping choices and concessions.

## E — 可执行步骤 (Execution)

当 skill 被激活后, agent 应按以下步骤执行:

1. **Collect**
   - 动作: Collect: gather their facts, values, fears, and desired outcomes.
   - 完成标准: 能产出可检查的文本、问题、判断或下一步。

2. **Summarize**
   - 动作: Summarize: combine paraphrase plus emotion labels in their language, not yours.
   - 完成标准: 能产出可检查的文本、问题、判断或下一步。

3. **Wait for signal**
   - 动作: Wait for signal: proceed only when they confirm, deepen, or visibly relax; otherwise refine.
   - 完成标准: 能产出可检查的文本、问题、判断或下一步。

## B — 边界 (Boundary) ★

### 不要在以下情况使用此 skill

- Do not chase “you’re right”; it can mean “stop talking.”
- Do not invent their worldview; use evidence from what they said.
- Do not use as a trick to bypass genuine disagreement.

### 作者在书中警告的失败模式

- 不要把谈判当成战斗、辩论或单纯的价格拉扯; 先发现真实约束。
- 不要追逐表面 “Yes”; 没有 “How” 的 yes 可能毫无执行力。

### 作者的盲点 / 时代局限

- 本书来自 FBI/危机谈判背景, 现实商务/亲密关系中权力、法律和长期关系约束更复杂。
- 书中战术很强, 但若脱离伦理边界, 容易滑向操控。

### 容易混淆的邻近方法论

- Use voss-labeling earlier when emotions are not yet clear.
- Use voss-bend-reality after “that’s right” when shaping choices and concessions.
