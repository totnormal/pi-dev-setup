# Resolve Where-to-Play / How-to-Win Fit

## R — Original Passage (Reading)

> Do consider how to win in concert with where to play. The choices should be mutually reinforcing, creating a strong strategic core for the company.
>
> — Source: Playing to Win — A.G. Lafley & Roger Martin, Where to Play; How to Win

---

## I — Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

Where-to-play and how-to-win are a pair. A market is attractive only if the company can win there; an advantage is meaningful only in a defined arena.

---

## A1 — Application in the Source Material (Past Application)

Playing to Win repeatedly shows choices as paired decisions, not standalone market-size exercises.

## B — Boundary

### Do not use this skill when

- Do not choose markets only by size.
- Do not choose generic advantages.
- Do not separate internal functions from strategic choices.

### Common corpus-level failure modes

- Replacing strategy with goals, slogans, or wish lists.
- Treating a case study or correlation as universal proof.
- Over-optimizing short-term metrics while starving long-term brand effects.
- Over-targeting existing customers while ignoring reach, penetration, and availability.
- Producing a plan with no capabilities, systems, owners, or review cadence.
