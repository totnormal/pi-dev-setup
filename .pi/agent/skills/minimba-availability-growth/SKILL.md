# Grow by Building Mental and Physical Availability

## R — Original Passage (Reading)

> Brands need distinctive assets irrespective of whether or not they are strongly differentiated from competitors.
>
> — Source: Marketing TEP — Byron Sharp; How Not to Plan — APG, Mental and physical availability

---

## I — Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

Sharp/Ehrenberg-Bass growth logic emphasizes making brands easy to think of and easy to buy. Mental and physical availability increase the chances of being chosen.

---

## A1 — Application in the Source Material (Past Application)

Marketing TEP teaches mental/physical availability; How Not to Plan emphasizes distribution, reach, distinctiveness, and loyalty limits.
