# Request Hetzner Tax Residence Certificate

## When to use
When you need an official tax residence certificate (Bescheinigung des steuerlichen Wohnsitzes) for Hetzner Online GmbH for a specific fiscal year (e.g., 2026) for tax treaty benefits, VAT reverse-charge, or other official purposes.

## Steps
1. Log in to your Hetzner account via Robot (https://robot.your-server.de/) or Hetzner Console (https://console.hetzner.com/).
2. Navigate to the support/ticket section.
   - Robot: Support → Create a ticket.
   - Console: Help & Support → New ticket.
3. Select the **Accounting** (or Billing/Accounting) department as the ticket category.
4. In the ticket description, include:
   - Your customer/account number (visible in top‑right of Robot/Console).
   - Clear request: “Please issue a tax residence certificate (Bescheinigung des steuerlichen Wohnsitzes) for Hetzner Online GmbH for the fiscal year 2026.”
   - Preferred language (German or English) if needed.
   - Optional: purpose of the certificate.
5. Submit the ticket.
6. Wait for a reply from the Accounting team (usually 1–2 business days). The certificate will be attached as a PDF.
7. Verify the PDF contains Hetzner’s full legal name, address, tax ID, and a statement of German tax residence for the requested year, signed/stamped by Hetzner’s accounting department.

## Pitfalls
- Do not search for a publicly downloadable PDF; Hetzner does not publish the certificate online.
- Ensure your account contact information is up‑to‑date; mismatched details can cause delays.
- If you need the certificate for multiple years, you can request each year separately or ask for a multi‑year statement in the same ticket.
- For urgent needs, phone Hetzner Support (see the Support Center for local numbers) and reference your ticket number.

## Verification
- After receiving the PDF, check that:
  - The issuer is “Hetzner Online GmbH”.
  - The address matches the registered office (e.g., Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany).
  - The tax ID (Steuernummer) is present.
  - The certificate explicitly states the fiscal year (e.g., 2026).
  - There is a signature, stamp, or official wording from the Accounting team.
