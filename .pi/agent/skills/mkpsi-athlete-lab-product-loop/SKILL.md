## I — Methodology skeleton (Interpretation)

- **Recognize:** Product improvements are abstract because testing is separated from high-intensity use.
  - **Intervene:** Put prototypes on demanding users, watch failures in context, record mechanics, and immediately send modifications to production.
  - **Assess:** Evaluate both user outcome and artifact behavior: comfort, wear, grip, injury, speed, and repeat preference.
- **2 source examples:**
  1. Bowerman tears apart Tigers, tracks arches/soles/toes/instep during races, and pushes Onitsuka toward an Americanized shoe.

- **Trigger phrases:** “We need real-world stress testing”; “Power users are modifying the product”; “The spec misses how bodies behave”; “Performance depends on small details.”
- **Sibling confusion risks:**

  - **V2:** Pair elite feedback with manufacturability and broader user comfort tests.
  - **V3:** Build a standing athlete/user lab that continuously feeds design, sourcing, and copy.
