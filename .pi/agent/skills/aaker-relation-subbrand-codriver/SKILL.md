# Subbrand & Co-Driver

## R — Reading

> "Subbrands are brands connected to a master or parent brand and augment or modify the associations of that master brand. ... the link between subbrands and their master brand is closer than the like between endorsers and the endorsed brands. Because of this closeness, a subbrand has considerable potential to affect the associations of the master brand, which in turn can be both a risk and an opportunity."
>
> — Aaker & Joachimsthaler (2000), p. 14

> "When both the master brand and the subbrand have major driver roles, it is considered a co-driver situation. ... Unless the two brands stand for comparable quality, the association might tarnish the more prestigious brand."
>
> — Aaker & Joachimsthaler (2000), p. 14–15

---

## I — Methodology skeleton (Interpretation)

A **subbrand** is tied tightly to the master and *augments/modifies* its associations (adds attribute, application, newness, personality, energy). Two modes:
- **Subbrand as driver** — master is primary frame; subbrand adds context (HP Deskjet, Dell Dimension, GE Capital vs GE Appliances, Ocean Spray Craisins, Club Med).
- **Co-driver** — master AND subbrand each have major driver role (Gillette + Mach3, Virgin + Virgin Vie). Usually requires the master already has credibility in the category.

**The closeness rule:** because the link is tighter than endorser↔endorsed, a subbrand can *move* the master's associations — upside (energy, relevance) and downside (dilution/tarnish). A co-driver that pairs unequal quality tarnishes the prestige brand; an *endorser* role (Marriott→Courtyard) protects it better than a co-driver would.

---

## A1 — Application in the source

### Case 1: Gillette + Mach3 (Co-driver)
- **Context**: Gillette already credible in razors; Mach3 is especially innovative.
- **Use**: both drive; neither dominates.
- **Result**: mutual loyalty/reinforcement.

### Case 2: Virgin + Virgin Vie (Co-driver)
- **Context**: Virgin brand older than cosmetics target; "Virgin Cosmetics" descriptor would be less credible.
- **Use**: Vie subbrand + young celebrity to separate from Richard Branson's Virgin.
- **Result**: credibility with twenty-somethings.

### Case 3: Marriott as co-driver would have risked it
- **Point**: if Marriott were co-driver (name as prominent as Courtyard), the premium master would look stretched down. Endorser role was safer.

---

## A2 — Future Trigger

1. Extending a master into a meaningful new segment while keeping the master visible.
2. Launching an offering where master + new name should *both* be salient.
3. Worried a tight subbrand link will drag or lift the master.

### Language signals
- "subbrand under [master]"
- "extend [master] into [segment]"
- "both the master and the new name should lead"
- "Gillette Mach3 style co-driver"

### Distinction from siblings
- vs `aaker-relation-endorsed-brands`: subbrand = *major* master driver role; endorsed = *minor*.
- vs `aaker-relation-branded-house`: subbrand keeps a named, driver sub-entity; branded-house reduces it to a descriptor.

---

## E — Execution steps

1. **Decide driver mode** — descriptor (branded house), subbrand-as-driver, or co-driver.
   - Done when: mode named + reason (master credibility in category?).
2. **Check association transfer** — will the subbrand *add* relevant attributes or *conflict* with the master?
   - Done when: a +/− association list exists.
3. **Quality-parity test (co-driver only)** — do both brands signal comparable quality? If not, downgrade to endorser (protect master).
4. **Name the subbrand for freedom** — a distinct subbrand (Virgin Vie) beats a descriptor ("Virgin Cosmetics") when the target differs.
5. **Plan master protection** — monitor for dilution; see aaker-relation-avoid-pitfalls.

---

## B — Boundary

### Don't use for
- Borrowing only light credibility (use aaker-relation-endorsed-brands).
- When the subbrand would clearly *conflict* with the master (use house-of-brands or endorsed).

### Failure modes warned by the authors
- Treating a subbrand like an endorsed brand when it should be freer (Revolutionary Lipcolor *from* Revlon vs *subbrand to* Revlon — less freedom).
- Co-driver pairing unequal quality → tarnishes prestige master.

### Author blind spots
- "Closeness" risk is amplified in social/media where a subbrand's misstep trends onto the master instantly.

### Easily confused with
- `aaker-relation-endorsed-brands` (the major/minor driver-role line) and `aaker-relation-branded-house`.
