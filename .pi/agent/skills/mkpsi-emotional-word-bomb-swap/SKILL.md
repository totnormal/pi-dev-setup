# Replace dead institutional words with emotionally resonant human words.

## R — Reading

> “Some words sparkle and sizzle, others fall flat.”
- **Atomic executable skill:** Scan a message for dead abstractions, then swap selected terms for simple, relational, high-association words such as we, you, love, great, dream, believe, imagine.
- **RIA skeleton:**
  - **Recognize:** Draft uses “framework,” “deliverables,” “benchmarking,” “accountability,” “collaboration,” or similar low-emotion terms.
  - **Intervene:** Replace with human pronouns, sensory/action words, and emotion-appropriate “word bombs.”
  - **Assess:** Message feels warmer, clearer, and more alive without losing precision.
- **2 source examples:**
  1. “I loved what you did this year. It was great. Really great” beats “performance was significantly above requirements.”
  2. “What makes great teaching” made an empirical pedagogy review more noticeable.
- **Trigger phrases:** “This sounds corporate”; “make it inspiring”; “too dry”; “needs more warmth”; “humanize this.”
- **Sibling confusion risks:** Not metaphor framing; not flattery; not empty hype.
- **Boundaries:** Use emotional words only where the emotion is intended; during sensitive layoffs/restructuring, lower-emotion language may be appropriate.
- **Validation notes:**
  - **V1:** Highlight low-emotion abstractions.
  - **V2:** Run before/after listener reaction.
  - **V3:** Track recall and affective descriptors.”
