# Pre-Suasion Attention Design

## R — Reading

> “By guiding preliminary attention strategically, it’s possible for a communicator to move recipients into agreement with a message before they experience it.”
>
> — Robert B. Cialdini, Chapters 1–9

---

## I — Methodology skeleton (Interpretation)

1. Pre-suasion changes the mental context before the request: what is focal becomes important, and what is focal is often treated as causal.
2. The opener should direct attention to a concept genuinely relevant to the later ask.
3. Useful openers include diagnostic questions, self-relevant cues, unfinished loops, environmental cues, and association-rich language.
4. Because it acts before conscious evaluation, every opener needs an ethical relevance check.

---

## A1 — Application in the source

### Case 1: Furniture website clouds vs pennies
- **Problem**: Background cues shifted shoppers toward comfort or price.
- **Methodology use**: Environmental associations changed what criterion felt important.
- **Result**: UX context should prime the actual decision criterion, not irrelevant mood.

### Case 2: French/German wine music
- **Problem**: Country music increased selection of same-country wines.
- **Methodology use**: Associative cues guided attention and choice.
- **Result**: Use associations only when relevant and transparent enough to defend.

   - Done when: the output names the evidence, the intended behavioral effect, and the ethical constraint.

## B — Boundary

### Do not use this skill when

- Do not use subliminal, irrelevant, or deceptive priming.
- Do not prime fear, sex, threat, or identity unless directly relevant and proportionate.
- If the later offer cannot withstand explicit scrutiny, do not pre-suade; fix the offer.

### Global Cialdini safety constraints

- Truthful evidence beats persuasion technique; if the evidence is weak, say so.
- Preserve meaningful choice; avoid hidden defaults, fake constraints, or social pressure that makes refusal unsafe.
- Name the influence mechanism in the answer so the user can judge it consciously.
