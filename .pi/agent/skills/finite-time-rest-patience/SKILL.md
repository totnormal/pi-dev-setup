# Practice Rest, Patience, and Staying on the Bus

## R — Original Passage (Reading)

> Three Principles of Patience.
>
> — Source: Four Thousand Weeks — Oliver Burkeman, Rediscovering Rest; The Impatience Spiral; Staying on the Bus

---

## I — Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

Rest and patience are not productivity fuel only; they are ways of inhabiting finite time without turning everything into instrumental output. Staying with a path long enough creates depth.

---

## A1 — Application in the Source Material (Past Application)

Burkeman connects pathological productivity, rules for rest, impatience, and staying on the bus to a humane alternative to frantic switching.
