# Ask the dumb question that exposes the real behavioral why.

## R — Reading

> “There are often two reasons behind people’s behaviour: the ostensibly logical reason, and the real reason.”

  - **Intervene:** Ask childish “why do people really do/hate/buy this?” questions, then list emotional, social, status, control, uncertainty, and identity explanations.
  - **Assess:** Test interventions against revealed behavior, not survey explanations.
- **2 source examples:**
  1. Toothpaste: if dental health were the sole reason, mint flavoring in 95% of toothpastes would be hard to explain.

## I — Methodology skeleton (Interpretation)

- **Recognize:** The team has accepted the official function as the motivation: food = hunger, toothpaste = dental health, train seats = comfort.
  - **Intervene:** Ask childish “why do people really do/hate/buy this?” questions, then list emotional, social, status, control, uncertainty, and identity explanations.
  - **Assess:** Test interventions against revealed behavior, not survey explanations.
- **2 source examples:**
  1. Toothpaste: if dental health were the sole reason, mint flavoring in 95% of toothpastes would be hard to explain.
  2. Restaurants: “hunger” is insufficient; their value lies in social connection and status.
- **Trigger phrases:** “Obviously they do it because…”; “Customers told us they want more seats”; “The purpose of this product is self-evident”; “Research says they care about outcomes.”
- **Sibling confusion risks:**
  - Not psychoanalysis of individuals; focus on population-level behavior patterns.
  - Not dismissing stated reasons as lies; people may sincerely report reasons they cannot fully access.
- **Boundaries:** Avoid manipulative exploitation of hidden vulnerabilities; use especially where current stated-preference research fails to predict behavior.
- **Validation notes:**
  - **V1:** Produce a “surface reason vs. possible real reasons” map and test one non-obvious reason.
  - **V2:** Add observational/revealed-preference evidence across contexts.
  - **V3:** Create a repeatable diagnostic for official function, emotional payoff, social signal, and self-signal.
