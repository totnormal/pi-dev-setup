# Restore the Human Side of Business

## R — Original Passage (Reading)

> Successful people and companies combine the story and the spreadsheet and by doing so restore the soul of business.
>
> — Source: Restoring the Soul of Business — Rishad Tobaccowala, Opening thesis; story and spreadsheet

---

## I — Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

Tobaccowala’s core frame is story plus spreadsheet: quantitative discipline matters, but business loses its soul when it forgets emotion, culture, trust, meaning, and human complexity.

---

## A1 — Application in the Source Material (Past Application)

The book argues that data-driven technologies create wealth and possibility but also polarization, trust breakdown, and dehumanized management when story is ignored.
