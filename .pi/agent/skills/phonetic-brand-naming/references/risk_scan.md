# Linguistic Risk Scan (Heuristic, Cautious)

This is not a substitute for native-speaker review or legal clearance. Use it to catch obvious issues early and to flag uncertainty.

## Inputs to Request (If Not Provided)

- Target regions (countries)
- Target languages (spoken and written)
- Any cultural sensitivities to avoid (religion, politics, taboo topics)

If the user does not specify, default to: "English-only heuristic scan" and flag that additional markets require additional checking.

## What to Scan For (Quick Checklist)

For each top candidate (and especially finalists):

- Negative meanings of the whole word in the target languages (if known)
- Negative meanings of obvious substrings/syllables
- Similarity in sound to common profanity/insults/taboo words (flag uncertainty)
- Unwanted homophones in the target language ("sounds like X")
- Awkward syllable breaks that create unintended words when spoken quickly
- Risky initials/acronyms created by the name + likely tagline (optional)

## How to Report Risk

Be explicit about certainty:

- "Likely safe" (no known issues in the languages you can reasonably assess)
- "Uncertain - needs native speaker check" (when you cannot assess confidently)
- "High risk" (obvious negative meaning or confusion risk)

When risk exists, propose:

- Spelling tweak (remove risky substring)
- Pronunciation tweak (change vowel/consonant)
- Alternate candidate that preserves the strategy direction

## Mandatory Next Steps (Always Include)

In the final deliverable, include a checklist:

1. Preliminary trademark search by region (professional tools or counsel)
2. Domain availability checks (common TLDs + country TLDs)
3. Native-speaker linguistic/cultural review for each key market
4. Legal counsel review before committing resources
