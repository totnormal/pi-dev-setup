# Candor Calibration (7-8/10)

## Target Tone

- Direct and specific.
- Evidence before opinion.
- Consequence-aware language.
- Constructive next step after each major critique.

## Avoid

- Personal judgments.
- Insults or mockery.
- Ambiguous hedging that hides the point.
- Absolute claims without evidence.

## Preferred Pattern

- Observation: what is visible.
- Impact: why it matters commercially.
- Fix: concrete action with effort/impact signal.

## Example Language

Use:
- "This visual cue signals X, which conflicts with your Y objective."
- "This is category-safe but low-distinctiveness; collision risk is high."
- "Keep A and B. Rebuild C because it weakens trust at conversion touchpoints."

Avoid:
- "This is terrible."
- "Looks bad."
- "I just don't like it."
