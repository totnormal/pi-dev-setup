# Intake Question Bank

Ask up to 12 high-leverage questions per round. Use multiple rounds if needed.

## Strategy

- What is the current positioning statement in one sentence?
- What promise must the identity communicate in the first 3 seconds?
- Which brand attributes are non-negotiable to signal visually?
- What proof points (RTBs) should visuals reinforce?
- Is the business trying to preserve equity or signal a strategic shift?

## Audience

- Who are primary and secondary segments?
- What trust cues does each segment require before buying?
- Which audience currently misunderstands the brand and why?
- Which audience should feel excluded by design (if any)?

## Category and Competitors

- What category are you selling in today, not aspirationally?
- Which competitors are most visually similar right now?
- Which brands must you not resemble?
- Which category codes are essential for credibility?
- Where do you want to conform vs disrupt category conventions?

## Assets and Touchpoints

- Provide links or files for logo suite, typography, palette, site/app, ads, social, packaging, guidelines.
- Which touchpoints drive most revenue and therefore matter most in this audit?
- Which current assets perform best and worst?
- Are there known consistency breakdowns between brand and product UI?

## Constraints

- Timeline, budget, and production constraints?
- Required markets/languages (including diacritics or RTL)?
- Accessibility baseline and legal constraints?
- What equity must be retained (logo shape, color memory, mascot, layout signature)?

## Brand Disambiguation Check

When starting from brand name only:
- I found these candidate profiles and domains. Which one is correct?
- Are there regional variants or sub-brands that should be included/excluded?
