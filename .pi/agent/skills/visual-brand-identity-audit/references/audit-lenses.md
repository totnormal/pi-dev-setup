# Audit Lenses

Use all applicable lenses. Tie every major claim to evidence.

## 1. Executive Snapshot

- What does this identity communicate in 3 seconds?
- Top 5 strengths.
- Top 5 risks.

## 2. Scorecard

Score `0-10` per dimension:
- Distinctiveness
- Clarity
- Coherence
- Flexibility
- Accessibility
- Category Fit
- Strategic Fit

Also assign:
- category importance `1-5`
- client importance `1-5`

Compute aggregated weighted score.

## 3. Design System and Architecture

- System parts: logo suite, type, color, layout/grid, iconography, imagery, motion, UI overlap.
- Standardized vs improvised rules.
- Scalability across touchpoints.

## 4. Visual Structure and Composition

- Hierarchy, rhythm, density, whitespace, alignment logic.
- Cropping/framing/focal conventions.
- Eye-path first-second-third.

## 5. Typography Semantics

- Classification and letterform traits.
- Readability by size and device.
- Personality implied by type choices.
- Pairing and hierarchy discipline.

## 6. Color System, Accessibility, Palette Intelligence

- Palette role assignment (primary/secondary/neutral/semantic/accent).
- Temperature, saturation, contrast behavior.
- WCAG 2.1 AA checks and color-blind risks.
- Tint/shade system and state-color governance.

## 7. Semiotics and Symbolism

- Signals: iconic, indexical, symbolic.
- Denotation vs connotation.
- Embedded metaphors.
- Cultural misreadings and landmines.

## 8. Brand Feel

- 10-word descriptor set.
- Emotional register (formal/casual, premium/value, calm/energetic).
- Texture/tactility cues.

## 9. Distinctiveness and Similarity Mapping

- “Looks like” archetype mapping.
- Points of parity and difference.
- Collision risks and whitespace opportunities.

## 10. Category Fit and Disruption

- Suitability to buying psychology and trust requirements.
- Which category codes are followed or broken.
- Recommended stance: conform / hybrid / disrupt.

## 11. Consumer Perception

- Predicted decoding by segment.
- First-impression expectations.
- Trust builders vs skepticism triggers.
- Inclusivity signals (invited/excluded).

## 12. Strategy and Marketing Alignment

- Traceability to positioning, messaging, RTBs.
- Funnel support: awareness, consideration, conversion, retention.
- Channel readiness: social, site hero, app tile, shelf, email, OOH.

## 13. Production and Scalability

- Print and packaging risks.
- Responsive digital behavior.
- Motion potential.
- Globalization and multilingual constraints.

## 14. Equity to Protect

- What must be retained for memory and continuity.
- What can change safely.

## 15. Prioritized Action Plan

- Quick Wins (1-2 weeks)
- System Fixes (1-2 months)
- Strategic Rebuild Options
- Include impact vs effort for each action.
