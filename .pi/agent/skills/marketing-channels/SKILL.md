# Marketing Channels — Platform & Channel Execution

## Extended Details

Complete reference for executing marketing across social, influencer, community,
growth, demand gen, B2B SaaS, local, video, brand, and ABM channels.

---

# Section 1: Social Media Marketing

## Platform Strategies

| Platform | Content Type | Best Practices | Posting Frequency |
|----------|-------------|----------------|-------------------|
| **LinkedIn** | Thought leadership, case studies, industry insights | Long-form text posts, carousel documents, native video | 3-5x/week |
| **Twitter/X** | Hot takes, threads, real-time commentary | Threads (5-10 tweets), polls, engagement replies | 1-3x/day |
| **Instagram** | Visual storytelling, behind-the-scenes | Reels (9:16), Stories, carousel posts, Guides | 3-5x/week |
| **TikTok** | Short-form entertainment, education | 9:16 vertical, hooks in first 2 seconds, trending audio | Daily |
| **Facebook** | Community groups, event promotion, retargeting | Video content, group engagement, paid ads | 2-3x/week |

## Content Calendar
- Plan 2-4 weeks ahead
- Mix: 40% educational, 30% entertaining, 20% promotional, 10% personal
- Repurpose top-performing content across platforms (adapt format, don't copy-paste)
- Track best posting times per platform

## Community Management
- Respond to comments within 4 hours
- Engage with other creators' content (comment, share)
- Build relationships, not just broadcast
- Use social listening tools for brand mentions

## Paid Social
- Retargeting: website visitors, engaged social followers, email subscribers
- Lookalike audiences: seed with best customers
- Creative testing: 3-5 variations per ad group
- Budget: start small, scale what works

## Metrics That Matter
- Engagement rate (not follower count)
- Click-through rate
- Social-sourced conversions (not just vanity metrics)
- Share of voice in your category

# Section 2: Influencer Marketing

## Influencer Tiers
| Tier | Followers | Cost | Best For |
|------|-----------|------|----------|
| **Mega** | 1M+ | $10K-100K+ | Brand awareness, mass reach |
| **Macro** | 100K-1M | $1K-10K | Awareness + credibility |
| **Mid-tier** | 10K-100K | $500-5K | Engagement + conversion |
| **Micro** | 1K-10K | $100-500 or product | High engagement, niche audiences |
| **Nano** | <1K | Product/exchange | Authentic advocacy |

## Influencer Vetting
- Engagement rate > 3% (micro-influencers may be 5-8%)
- Audience demographics match your ICP
- Brand safety: review past content for conflicts
- Authenticity: check for fake followers/bought engagement
- Previous brand partnerships: were they authentic or forced?

## Campaign Types
- Sponsored posts (single piece of content)
- Ambassador programs (ongoing partnership)
- Affiliate/referral (performance-based)
- Product seeding (free product, no guarantee)
- Co-created content (joint product/content development)

## Campaign Brief Template
```
Campaign: [Name]
Influencer: [Handle/name]
Deliverables: [Number and type of content]
Timeline: [Start and end dates]
Key messages: [2-3 talking points]
Mandatories: [Required elements: tags, links, disclosures]
Do-not-say: [Restricted messages]
Compensation: [Fee, product, affiliate rate]
Usage rights: [Repurpose permissions]
```

## Measurement
- Track unique discount codes per influencer
- UTM parameters for all links
- Measure: reach, engagement, clicks, conversions, CPA
- ROI: (revenue from influencer - cost) / cost

# Section 3: Community Building

## Community Platform Selection
| Platform | Best For | Size Range |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| **Discord** | Developer communities, gaming, crypto | 100-10,000+ |
| **Reddit** | Topic-based, anonymous discussion | 1,000-1,000,000+ |
| **Circle** | Paid communities, SaaS customer communities | 100-5,000 |
| **Slack** | Professional, invite-only | 50-5,000 |
| **Facebook Groups** | Broad audiences, local communities | 1,000-100,000+ |
| **LinkedIn Groups** | B2B professional communities | 500-10,000 |

## Engagement Programming
- Weekly discussion threads
- Monthly AMA / expert sessions
- User spotlight features
- Challenges and competitions
- Member-to-member introductions

## Ambassador Program
- Identify top 5% most engaged members
- Invite to private ambassador group
- Give early access to features/content
- Feature their content prominently
- Provide exclusive swag/perks

## Community Metrics
- Active members (weekly/monthly)
- Posts per member per week
- Response time to questions
- Member retention rate
- Net Promoter Score

## Moderation Framework
- Clear community guidelines
- Progressive discipline: warning → temporary suspension → ban
- Recruit volunteer moderators from top members
- Celebrate positive behavior publicly

# Section 4: Growth Marketing

## The Growth Framework
1. **Analyze**: Identify bottlenecks in the funnel
2. **Ideate**: Generate hypotheses to move the metric
3. **Prioritize**: ICE score (Impact × Confidence / Ease)
4. **Test**: Run controlled experiment
5. **Learn**: Document results, decide: scale, iterate, or kill
6. **Repeat**: Weekly growth cadence

## Funnel Analysis (AARRR Framework)
- **Acquisition**: How do users find you? (channels, CAC)
- **Activation**: First positive experience (time to aha moment)
- **Retention**: Do they come back? (D7, D30 retention curves)
- **Referral**: Do they tell others? (NPS, referral rate)
- **Revenue**: Do they pay? (conversion rate, ARPU, LTV)

## Viral Loop Design
- **Viral coefficient (K)**: Invitations sent × conversion rate
- K > 1 = exponential growth; K < 1 = linear growth
- Built-in virality: collaboration features, sharing, referrals
- Incentivized virality: referral bonuses, unlock features

## Experimentation Process
- Weekly growth meetings to review experiments
- Test one variable at a time
- Minimum sample size for statistical significance
- Document all experiments (even failed ones) in a growth log

# Section 5: Demand Generation

## Demand Gen Funnel
1. **Awareness**: Content, PR, events, social → reach target audience
2. **Education**: Webinars, guides, newsletters → build understanding
3. **Consideration**: Case studies, demos, free trials → prove value
4. **Conversion**: Sales conversations, proposals → close deals

## Campaign Types
- Thought leadership content (research reports, original data)
- Webinars and virtual events
- Email nurture campaigns
- Account-based campaigns (see ABM section)
- Partner co-marketing
- PR and media relations

## Lead Scoring Model (for Demand Gen)
| Signal | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Visited pricing page | +10 |
| Downloaded content | +5 |
| Attended webinar | +15 |
| Started trial | +20 |
| Requested demo | +25 |
| Fit criteria met (ICP) | +20 |
| No engagement in 30 days | -10 |

# Section 6: B2B SaaS Marketing

## Key Metrics
- **MRR/ARR growth**: Month-over-month revenue growth
- **CAC payback**: Months to recover customer acquisition cost (<12 months)
- **LTV:CAC ratio**: Target > 3:1
- **Expansion revenue**: Upsell + cross-sell as % of total revenue
- **Net revenue retention**: > 100% (expansion > churn)

## Best Practices
- Content marketing (SEO blog) is the primary channel for most B2B SaaS
- Free tool/calculator for lead generation
- Product-led trials to reduce CAC
- Customer case studies as primary social proof
- Analyst relations (Gartner, Forrester) for enterprise credibility
- Integration partnerships for distribution

# Section 7: Local Marketing

## Google Business Profile
- Complete all fields (business description, categories, hours, photos)
- Post weekly updates
- Respond to all reviews (within 24 hours)
- Add products/services
- Upload 10+ quality photos

## Local Content Strategy
- City/neighborhood-specific blog posts
- Local event coverage and sponsorships
- Local news mentions and PR
- "Best of" lists including your business

## Multi-Location Marketing
- Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across all locations
- Local landing pages with unique content per location
- City-specific social media presence
- Local team member spotlights

## Community Marketing
- Sponsor local events and sports teams
- Partner with complementary local businesses
- Host local workshops or meetups
- Engage in local Facebook groups, Nextdoor

## Local Advertising
- Google Local Services Ads
- Geofenced social media ads
- Local radio or print (still effective for some demographics)
- Direct mail to targeted postal codes

# Section 8: Video Marketing

## Video Content Types
| Type | Purpose | Length | Platform |
|------|---------|--------|----------|
| **Explainer** | Product/service intro | 60-120s | YouTube, website |
| **Tutorial** | How-to education | 3-10 min | YouTube, blog embed |
| **Testimonial** | Social proof | 60-90s | Website, landing pages |
| **Behind-the-scenes** | Brand personality | 15-60s | Instagram, TikTok |
| **Webinar recording** | Authority building | 20-60 min | YouTube, gated |
| **Shorts/Reels** | Reach and awareness | 15-60s | TikTok, IG, YT Shorts |

## Video SEO
- Keyword in title (first 60 characters)
- Detailed description (200+ words) with keywords
- Chapters/timestamps for longer videos
- Custom thumbnail with high contrast and text
- Closed captions for accessibility and SEO

## Production Workflow
1. **Script**: Write before recording (even for short-form)
2. **Hook** (0:00-0:10): Grab attention immediately
3. **Intro** (0:10-0:30): State the promise/value
4. **Main content**: Deliver on the promise
5. **Summary**: Recap key points
6. **CTA**: What to do next (subscribe, visit site, watch another)

# Section 9: Brand Marketing

## Brand Strategy Framework
1. **Purpose**: Why does the brand exist beyond making money?
2. **Position**: Where does the brand sit in the competitive landscape?
3. **Personality**: If the brand were a person, who would it be?
4. **Promise**: What does the brand guarantee to deliver?

## Brand Identity Elements
- Logo and variations
- Color palette (primary, secondary, accent)
- Typography (headings, body, accent)
- Imagery style (photography, illustration, iconography)
- Voice and tone guidelines

## Brand Voice (NNGroup 4-Dimension Framework)
| Dimension | Scale | Example Brands |
|-----------|-------|---------------|
| Funny → Serious | Choose position | MailChimp (funny) → Stripe (serious) |
| Formal → Casual | Choose position | IBM (formal) → Slack (casual) |
| Respectful → Irreverent | Choose position | Harvard (respectful) → Wendy's (irreverent) |
| Enthusiastic → Matter-of-fact | Choose position | Disney (enthusiastic) → Apple (matter-of-fact) |

## Brand Metrics
- Unaided awareness (% who name you unprompted)
- Brand sentiment (positive/negative mentions ratio)
- Brand search volume (people searching for your brand name)
- Share of voice vs. competitors

# Section 10: Account-Based Marketing (ABM)

## ABM Tier Framework
| Tier | Accounts | Strategy | Personalization |
|------|----------|----------|----------------|
| **Strategic (1:1)** | 1-10 accounts | Dedicated campaigns per account | Hyper-personalized |
| **Scale (1:few)** | 10-100 accounts | Clustered by persona/industry | Semi-personalized |
| **Programmatic (1:many)** | 100-1000 accounts | Automated with account-level targeting | Segment-level |

## Account Selection Framework
1. **ICP fit**: Firmographic match
2. **Intent signals**: Research activity, technology changes, job postings
3. **Engagement**: Existing awareness (website visits, content downloads)
4. **Revenue potential**: Deal size, expansion opportunity

## Multi-Threading Strategy
- Identify 5-7 key stakeholders per account
- Map: economic buyer, champion, technical evaluator, end user
- Create content tailored to each role
- Coordinate outreach timing across stakeholders

## Engagement Scoring
| Signal | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Target page visits | High |
| Content downloads | Medium |
| Email engagement | Medium |
| Event attendance | High |
| Referral from existing customer | Very High |

## Measurement
- Account engagement score (composite metric)
- Pipeline created from target accounts
- Deal velocity vs. non-ABM deals
- Revenue from target accounts
