# Use the Playing to Win Choice Cascade

## R — Original Passage (Reading)

> What is your winning aspiration? Where will you play? How will you win? What capabilities must be in place? What management systems are required?
>
> — Source: Playing to Win — A.G. Lafley & Roger Martin, Strategy choice cascade

---

## I — Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

Strategy is an integrated set of reinforcing choices. The cascade forces trade-offs and connects ambition to arenas, advantage, capabilities, and systems.

---

## A1 — Application in the Source Material (Past Application)

Lafley and Martin use P&G examples to show how brand/category/channel/geography choices become executable when paired with capabilities and systems.

## B — Boundary

### Do not use this skill when

- Do not accept “serve everyone.”
- Do not define “win” as “be better.”
- Do not ignore capabilities/systems.

### Common corpus-level failure modes

- Replacing strategy with goals, slogans, or wish lists.
- Treating a case study or correlation as universal proof.
- Over-optimizing short-term metrics while starving long-term brand effects.
- Over-targeting existing customers while ignoring reach, penetration, and availability.
- Producing a plan with no capabilities, systems, owners, or review cadence.

Before finalizing output, run the final verification step in this skill. For larger artifacts, also apply `minimba-output-verifier`.
