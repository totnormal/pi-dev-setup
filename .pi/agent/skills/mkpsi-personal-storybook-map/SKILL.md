# Map life events to leadership philosophies.

## R — Reading

> “Every leader should have their own leadership storybook.”
- **Atomic executable skill:** Create a reusable bank of first-person stories by charting life highs/lows, listing core philosophies, and matching each philosophy to a vivid formative event.
- **RIA skeleton:**
  - **Recognize:** Speaker needs authenticity, vulnerability, or proof of values.
  - **Intervene:** Life timeline → top ten philosophies → match event to philosophy → draft sensory story.
  - **Assess:** Story shows why the speaker believes the principle, rather than merely asserting it.
- **2 source examples:**
  1. Jeff Bezos’s “harder to be kind than clever” story from childhood.
  2. Steve Jobs’s Stanford address built around adoption, being fired, and mortality.
- **Trigger phrases:** “Make it more authentic”; “What’s your leadership story?”; “Why do you care?”; “Tell us about yourself.”
- **Sibling confusion risks:** Not generic storytelling; not memoir; stories must serve a leadership point.
- **Boundaries:** Share only stories the speaker owns and can tell safely; avoid oversharing that hijacks the audience’s needs.
- **Validation notes:**
  - **V1:** At least one story per core philosophy.
  - **V2:** Audience identifies the intended value.
  - **V3:** Story becomes reusable without sounding scripted or exploitative.”
