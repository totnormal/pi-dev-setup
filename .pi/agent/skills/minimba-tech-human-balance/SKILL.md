# Balance Technology, Data, and Human Judgment

## R — Original Passage (Reading)

> The sweet siren call of optimizing numbers had led us to overlook the collateral damage of forgetting the human side of the equation.
>
> — Source: Restoring the Soul of Business — Rishad Tobaccowala, Technology, data, and human side

---

## I — Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

The skill asks where data/technology improves decisions and where it flattens meaning, trust, creativity, or human judgment. It turns digital transformation into a balanced design problem.

---

## A1 — Application in the Source Material (Past Application)

Tobaccowala discusses AI, 5G, screens, platforms, trust breakdown, and the need to extract meaning from data while seeing poetry in the plumbing.
