# Escape the Efficiency Trap

## R — Original Passage (Reading)

> The Efficiency Trap.
>
> — Source: Four Thousand Weeks — Oliver Burkeman, The Efficiency Trap

---

## I — Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

Efficiency often increases capacity only to invite more demands. The escape is not perfect control but choosing limits, refusing infinite queues, and measuring life by what matters rather than what gets processed.

---

## A1 — Application in the Source Material (Past Application)

Burkeman describes Sisyphus’s inbox, bottomless bucket lists, and the futility of clearing the decks before living.
