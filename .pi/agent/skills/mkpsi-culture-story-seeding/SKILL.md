# Shape culture by circulating true origin and behavior stories.

## R — Reading

> “Cultures are little more than collections of stories.”
- **Atomic executable skill:** Replace abstract culture claims with repeatable stories about origins, decisions, heroes, customer impact, and behaviors worth copying.
- **RIA skeleton:**
  - **Recognize:** Culture work is expressed as values posters, slogans, or internal-comms spin.
  - **Intervene:** Collect foundation stories and current case studies; retell them in leader walkabouts and meetings.
  - **Assess:** Employees retell the stories organically and infer the desired behavior.
- **2 source examples:**
  1. Innocent Drinks’ festival stall and yes/no bins encode entrepreneurial origin values.
  2. Greg Dyke’s BBC stories — news time shift, atrium reopening, personal email replies — signaled challenge and risk-taking.
- **Trigger phrases:** “Culture change”; “bring values to life”; “internal comms campaign”; “negative stories are spreading.”
- **Sibling confusion risks:** Related to personal stories, but focuses on group identity and norms, not individual authenticity.
- **Boundaries:** Stories must be true enough to withstand scrutiny; avoid centrally manufactured “spin” that employees discount.
- **Validation notes:**
  - **V1:** Inventory existing positive/negative stories.
  - **V2:** Seed three behavior stories through leaders.
  - **V3:** Track organic retelling and behavior signals.”

## I — Methodology skeleton (Interpretation)

- **Recognize:** Culture work is expressed as values posters, slogans, or internal-comms spin.
  - **Intervene:** Collect foundation stories and current case studies; retell them in leader walkabouts and meetings.
  - **Assess:** Employees retell the stories organically and infer the desired behavior.
- **2 source examples:**
  1. Innocent Drinks’ festival stall and yes/no bins encode entrepreneurial origin values.
  2. Greg Dyke’s BBC stories — news time shift, atrium reopening, personal email replies — signaled challenge and risk-taking.
- **Trigger phrases:** “Culture change”; “bring values to life”; “internal comms campaign”; “negative stories are spreading.”
- **Sibling confusion risks:** Related to personal stories, but focuses on group identity and norms, not individual authenticity.
- **Boundaries:** Stories must be true enough to withstand scrutiny; avoid centrally manufactured “spin” that employees discount.
- **Validation notes:**
  - **V1:** Inventory existing positive/negative stories.
  - **V2:** Seed three behavior stories through leaders.
  - **V3:** Track organic retelling and behavior signals.
