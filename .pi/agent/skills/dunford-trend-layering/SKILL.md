# Layer a Relevant Trend Without Muddying Positioning

## R — Original Passage (Reading)

> It’s always better to be a little boring than completely baffling.
>
> — April Dunford, Step 9: Layer On a Trend

---

## I — Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

Trends can create urgency, but they are not substitutes for category. Dunford’s guardrail is to first make the product and market obvious, then layer a trend only if it intersects with the product’s strengths and the customer’s priorities. The trend should explain why now, not obscure what the product is.

---

## A1 — Application in the Book (Past Application)

Redgate stayed in database tools but layered “database DevOps” onto its position as DevOps became important to development teams. Long Blockchain shows the failure mode: attaching a hot trend without a credible product link.

## B — Boundary

### Do not use this skill when

- Do not use a trend as the category.
- Do not add hype words solely for attention.
- Do not let content about the trend drift away from the product being sold.

### Common failure modes from Dunford's books

- Treating positioning as copywriting rather than strategic context.
- Talking about features before clarifying alternatives and value.
- Using generic trends, generic problems, or generic proof.
- Letting the buyer infer the purchase criteria alone.
