# Plan Media for Reach, Attention, and Effectiveness

## R — Original Passage (Reading)

> Channels with broad reach form natural candidates for brand building.
>
> — Source: The Long and the Short of It; Marketing TEP; How Not to Plan, Media reach and effectiveness

---

## I — Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

Media should match the job. Brand building typically needs broad reach, attention, emotion, memory, and time; activation can be narrower and immediate.

## A1 — Application in the Source Material (Past Application)
