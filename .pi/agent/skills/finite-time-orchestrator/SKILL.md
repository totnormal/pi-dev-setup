# Finite-Time Orchestrator

## R — Original Passage (Reading)

> In the long run, we’re all dead.
>
> — Source: Four Thousand Weeks — Oliver Burkeman, Corpus synthesis

---

## I — Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

The orchestrator reframes time management as finite-life choice management. It routes away from maximizing throughput and toward choosing what to neglect, protecting attention, accepting limits, and doing the next necessary thing.

---

## A1 — Application in the Source Material (Past Application)

Burkeman’s book moves from finitude and the efficiency trap through attention, rest, patience, shared time, cosmic insignificance, and practical tools for embracing limits.
