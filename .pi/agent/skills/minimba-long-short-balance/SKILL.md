# Balance Long-Term Brand Building and Short-Term Activation

## R — Original Passage (Reading)

> With the how category expenditure divides (typically 60:40): the optimum balance between long and short-term effects varies by category.
>
> — Source: The Long and the Short of It — Les Binet & Peter Field, Balancing short and long-term strategy

---

## I — Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

Sales activation works quickly and decays quickly. Brand building takes longer and compounds. The plan needs both, with distinct metrics and time horizons.

---

## A1 — Application in the Source Material (Past Application)

Binet and Field show broad-reach emotional/fame campaigns drive long-term effects while activation captures immediate demand.

## B — Boundary

### Do not use this skill when

- Do not apply 60/40 mechanically.
- Do not measure brand building only short term.
- Do not ignore activation.

### Common corpus-level failure modes

- Replacing strategy with goals, slogans, or wish lists.
- Treating a case study or correlation as universal proof.
- Over-optimizing short-term metrics while starving long-term brand effects.
- Over-targeting existing customers while ignoring reach, penetration, and availability.
- Producing a plan with no capabilities, systems, owners, or review cadence.
