# Company Research Agent

## Extended Details



Use this skill when you need a structured company research workflow that gathers sources, synthesizes findings, and produces a concise brief.

## What it does

- Collects company facts from web, docs, and other sources
- Organizes findings into research categories
- Produces a summary brief or report

## Good fits

- Company diligence
- Competitive and market research
- Internal briefing docs
