# Choose What to Neglect

## R — Original Passage (Reading)

> The Art of Creative Neglect.
>
> — Source: Four Thousand Weeks — Oliver Burkeman, Becoming a Better Procrastinator; Creative Neglect

---

## I — Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

Because time is finite, procrastination is inevitable. The question is whether neglect is unconscious and fear-driven or deliberate and values-driven.

---

## A1 — Application in the Source Material (Past Application)

Burkeman argues for becoming a better procrastinator: choosing what to neglect so important work can receive real attention.
