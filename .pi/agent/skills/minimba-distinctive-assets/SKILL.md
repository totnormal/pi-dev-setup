# Build and Use Distinctive Brand Assets

## R — Original Passage (Reading)

> Rather than striving for meaningful perceived differentiation, marketers should seek meaningless distinctiveness.
>
> — Source: Marketing TEP — Byron Sharp; How Not to Plan — APG, Distinctive assets

---

## I — Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

Distinctive assets are memory cues. They make the brand easier to notice and recognize, even when buyers do not perceive deep differentiation.

---

## A1 — Application in the Source Material (Past Application)

How Not to Plan contrasts distinctiveness with overclaimed differentiation; Marketing TEP treats assets as contributors to mental availability.

## B — Boundary

### Do not use this skill when

- Do not confuse assets with strategic differentiation.
- Do not change them because the team is bored.
- Do not rely on assets buyers do not know without a build plan.

### Common corpus-level failure modes

- Replacing strategy with goals, slogans, or wish lists.
- Treating a case study or correlation as universal proof.
- Over-optimizing short-term metrics while starving long-term brand effects.
- Over-targeting existing customers while ignoring reach, penetration, and availability.
- Producing a plan with no capabilities, systems, owners, or review cadence.
