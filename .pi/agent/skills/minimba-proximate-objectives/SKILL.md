# Set Proximate Objectives

## R — Original Passage (Reading)

> One of a leader’s most powerful tools is the creation of a good proximate objective.
>
> — Source: Good Strategy/Bad Strategy — Richard Rumelt, Chapter 7: Proximate Objectives

---

## I — Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

A proximate objective is near enough to be feasible and clear enough to coordinate action while still advancing the larger strategy.

---

## A1 — Application in the Source Material (Past Application)

Rumelt shows how leaders reduce ambiguity by setting objectives that teams can pursue now, generating learning and options.

## B — Boundary

### Do not use this skill when

- Do not create vanity milestones.
- Do not use before diagnosis exists.
- Do not make objectives impossible or trivial.

### Common corpus-level failure modes

- Replacing strategy with goals, slogans, or wish lists.
- Treating a case study or correlation as universal proof.
- Over-optimizing short-term metrics while starving long-term brand effects.
- Over-targeting existing customers while ignoring reach, penetration, and availability.
- Producing a plan with no capabilities, systems, owners, or review cadence.
