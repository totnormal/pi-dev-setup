# Accept Finitude and Do the Next Necessary Thing

## R — Original Passage (Reading)

> The Next Most Necessary Thing.
>
> — Source: Four Thousand Weeks — Oliver Burkeman, Facing Finitude; The Human Disease; The Next Most Necessary Thing

---

## I — Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

Accepting limits means giving up the fantasy of someday being fully in control. The practical move is to choose the next necessary thing and act inside uncertainty.

---

## A1 — Application in the Source Material (Past Application)

Burkeman ends with questions about comfort, impossible standards, accepting who you are, acting before you know what you are doing, and doing the next most necessary thing.
